Special Assessment Calculators

Some Concord property owners pay a yearly fee, called a special assessment, for local landscaping and street lights. Each of the four districts below uses its own formula. Enter facts about a property to see how its fee is figured.

3

Downtown Landscape Maintenance District

Downtown has four benefit zones, and they can overlap. A property pays a charge for each zone it is in, and the charges are added together. Depending on the zone, a charge is based on lot size, number of homes, or frontage (the length of the property's edge along the street).

Your property

Use 0 if the property does not face a landscaped median on a collector street.
Use 0 if the property does not face a landscaped median on a thoroughfare.
Formula

Each median zone splits its cost two ways. One-third is shared by the properties that face the median, based on frontage. Two-thirds is shared by every property in the zone, based on lot size.

How the rates go up each year

Each year, the rates can rise as much as prices rose, measured by the Consumer Price Index (CPI). The increase can't be more than 5%. This year's rates above already include the increase.

Citywide Street Lighting Assessment District
